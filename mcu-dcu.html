Sources & methodology

What's measured

Worldwide gross is lifetime theatrical box office — every dollar of ticket sales, domestic and international combined. It is revenue, not profit: studios typically keep roughly half of domestic ticket revenue and a smaller share internationally, after the exhibitor's cut.

Production budget covers negotiated above- and below-the-line filmmaking costs. It excludes marketing and P&A (prints and advertising) spend, which for a tentpole release commonly runs 50–100% of the production budget again.

Why figures vary by source

Studios rarely confirm final budgets, so tracking sites triangulate from insurance filings, trade reports, and tax-credit disclosures — figures for the same film can differ by tens of millions between outlets. Where a budget is genuinely contested, it's marked estimate in the explorer table.

A note on Spider-Man: Brand New Day

Released July 31, 2026, it is still in theatrical release as of this update. Its worldwide gross is current, not final, and will continue to rise before the run ends.
